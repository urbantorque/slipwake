SLIPWAKE / TIDELINE 0.50.0

Extract the complete ZIP. Open index.html in Chrome or Edge. Keep game.js and the audio folder beside it. All music and artwork work offline.

RACE THE COAST
Start with a short ride, then a three-race Cup: opening water, a corner test and a finale. Seven destinations, six rivals, three hulls and six permanent fittings. Finish a race to earn a fitting choice. Equipment, paints, custom controls, unfinished Cups and historical records remain saved on this device.

READ. COMMIT. RELEASE.
Steering builds progressively on every hull. Hold drift through the bend, then release into the open exit to catch the stern and carry speed. A brief earned release window protects a clean exit. Carry drift speed into a Wake burst to preserve part of the remaining boost. Follow a rival's mint trail, fill Wake, then steer into a clear side to pass. Hits cost speed but leave steering available. Battery preserves a burst through the bend; release into settled water to spend it.

YOUR BOAT
Drag the boat to inspect it, or focus it and use arrow keys. Select a fitting to preview its mechanism and driving tradeoff. Choose a fitted slot, then Fit. Test current and preview setups in the same short section. Try every hull, including locked hulls, on the same stock sea trial before choosing an unlock. Hulls, equipment, paint and saved boats stay in the Boathouse. Cup setup is optional.

CONTROLS
Keyboard: A/D or arrows steer; W/Up accelerates; E drifts; Q uses an item; Down slows; R recovers; Escape pauses. Rebind keys in Settings. Touch uses automatic throttle by default, with steer, Slow and Drift buttons. Controller support is retained: left stick, RT throttle, LT slow, A/LB drift, X/RB item, Y recover, Start pause.

COMBAT
Cannon spreads three shots, or focus it with Slow. Shoot bombs to disarm them. Bomb drops behind, or lobs ahead with Slow. Repulsor blocks and can return frontal fire. Slow vents Overdrive and clears weapons nearby. Practice supplies short item drills.

GRAPHICS & ACCESSIBILITY
Low, Balanced and High share readable course landmarks. Reduced motion, high contrast, large touch controls, automatic throttle and separate music/effect levels are in Settings. A stalled launch can retry or switch to Low without losing progress. The optional local scene probe is separate from live-race performance.

RECORDS & AUDIO
Changed race and handling rules use tideline-25 and tideline-trial-12. Historical ghosts remain stored, but only current rules pace a new race. Existing recorded destination and club arrangements, source provenance and licences are preserved. See AUDIO-CREDITS.txt and THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES.txt.
